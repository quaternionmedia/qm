## Standing constraints, in force

- **Autonomous agents are launched only on a direct human request**, with an
  audit block: `(start, predicted end, kill time, purpose)`. Nothing is launched
  unprompted.
- **Deterministic, composable scripts are the default.** Agent fan-out is
  break-glass, or a calculated approach with a stated reason a script cannot
  answer the question.
- **Governance names no product.** Invariants in `AGENTS.md`; vendor glue in
  `adapters/`, optional; nothing vendor-specific in `project-seed/`.
- `CLAUDE.md` symlinked to `AGENTS.md` is accepted. `.claude/commands` inside the
  seed is not.
