## What titanharvest is, in one paragraph

A Godot 4.7.2 prototype that lived in `Documents/titanharvest` without version
control, with design notes in an Obsidian vault and a third document pasted by a
collaborator. It is now a repository mirroring golfVs's shape — the docs trio,
`CODEOWNERS`, a docs-coupling gate and gdUnit4 headless in CI, an engine pin
file, LFS for binaries, a dev loop that refuses any engine but the pinned one —
with all three sources kept verbatim and a design that names the five things
they disagree on and decides none. Its own `docs/HANDOFF.md` is where its state
lives; this page only says it exists and why the roster now carries it.
